1. Energy signals (real-life examples)

Idea:
An energy signal has finite total energy and usually exists for a limited time or dies out.

Mathematically (no stress): total energy is finite.

Real-life examples
🔊 A clap or shout

You clap once → sound exists briefly → fades away

Total sound energy is limited
✅ Energy signal

💡 Camera flash

Very bright

Very short duration

Then… nothing
✅ Energy signal

📶 A single data packet sent over Wi-Fi

Packet starts

Packet ends

Energy used is finite
✅ Energy signal

⚡ Lightning strike

Huge intensity

Extremely short time
✅ Energy signal

🎵 A recorded song (played once)

Starts at time 0

Ends after 3–4 minutes
✅ Energy signal

2. Power signals (real-life examples)

Idea:
A power signal lasts for a very long time (theoretically infinite) and has finite average power, not finite total energy.

Real-life examples
🔌 Household AC voltage (230V / 120V)

Keeps oscillating forever (as long as the grid is on)

Average power is constant
✅ Power signal

📻 Radio carrier waves

Radio stations transmit continuously

Sinusoidal signals that don’t “end”
✅ Power signal

🕒 Clock signal in computers

Square wave running nonstop

Used to synchronize circuits
✅ Power signal

☀️ Sunlight (approximation)

Continuous electromagnetic radiation

Over very long time spans
✅ Treated as a power signal

3. Quick intuition shortcut 🧠

Ask yourself:

Does the signal end?

✔️ Yes → Energy signal

❌ No (keeps going) → Power signal

4. Do “pure” power signals really exist?

In the real world:

Nothing is truly infinite

But many signals last long enough that we model them as power signals

So:

Energy signals → very common & physically finite

Power signals → idealized, but excellent approximations for real systems





| Type              | Exists for      | Total Energy | Average Power |
| ----------------- | --------------- | ------------ | ------------- |
| **Energy signal** | short duration  | finite       | 0             |
| **Power signal**  | long/continuous | infinite     | finite        |
